Document search & regulatory AI

The most mature category. Nuclear plants carry billions of pages of regulatory and technical documentation — the clearest, most fundable pain point.

Atomic CanyonLive deployment

Its Neutron platform searches and interprets complex nuclear documents — scanned reports, handwritten notes, operational histories — trained on millions of pages of NRC data. The underlying FERMI embedding models were built with Oak Ridge National Lab on the Frontier supercomputer and open-sourced on Hugging Face.

In the fieldRunning at PG&E's Diablo Canyon — the first on-site generative-AI deployment at a U.S. nuclear plant. Cuts document search from hours to seconds.
Nuclearn65+ reactors

An AI platform built by former Palo Verde plant staff, automating compliance-heavy plant work: outage prep, maintenance planning, documentation and reporting. Products include AtomAssist, CAP AI and Engineering AI, trained on nuclear-specific data and integrating with systems like Maximo and SAP.

ReachDeployed across dozens of reactors in the U.S., Canada and the U.K. Secure, on-premise, and Part 810 export-control compliant.
02

Construction & lifecycle platforms

Aimed at the bigger bottleneck — building reactors on time and on budget. In 30 years the U.S. has completed only two new plants (Vogtle 3 & 4), both over budget.

Palantir × The Nuclear CompanyNOS

A jointly built "nuclear operating system" on Palantir's Foundry, unifying siloed construction, supply-chain and workforce data to speed reactor builds. The Nuclear Company is targeting a 6 GW fleet of large plants by the mid-2030s.

AngleSchedule certainty and cost control via sensors, digital twins and AI-powered document validation.
Microsoft × NVIDIAAI for nuclear

An end-to-end collaboration combining generative AI, digital-twin simulation and NVIDIA's Omniverse to streamline permitting, design and operations. Generative AI drafts and gap-checks the tens of thousands of pages a regulatory submission requires.

EcosystemStartups like Everstar and Atomic Canyon plug into Azure; Atomic Canyon's Neutron is on the Microsoft Marketplace.

Industrial incumbents

The large engineering firms embedding AI into existing plant operations rather than building standalone products.

ABB · GE Vernova · Siemens Energy · Hitachi

Integrating AI into utility-scale operations: predictive maintenance, anomaly detection and operational optimization. Hitachi has introduced a metaverse platform pairing AI with digital twins for plant maintenance and asset management.

FocusReactor safety, efficiency and asset-lifecycle management across operating fleets.

The pattern for NeutrAI

Almost every player that reached a real deployment picked one narrow, painful problem — document search, construction scheduling, maintenance — and owned it, rather than pitching "AI for all of nuclear." The open lane tends to sit where a specific workflow is still manual, data is fragmented, and regulatory stakes make accuracy worth paying for.
