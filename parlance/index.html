parlance
/ˈpɑː.ləns/ · noun, uncountable
  1. A particular way of speaking or of using words, especially one shared by a group. “In legal parlance, this is called consideration.”
  2. Everyday speech. “What is known in common parlance as a white lie.”
Origin: late 16th century, from Old French parler, “to speak”.
