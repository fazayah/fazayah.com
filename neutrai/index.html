FAQ

Common questions

Is NeutrAI acceptable for NRC submittals?
NeutrAI outputs are analysis aids and technical references. All safety-related calculations must be reviewed and approved by licensed engineers per 10 CFR 50 Appendix B. Audit trails and QA documentation to support that review are planned features.
How is sensitive plant data protected?
The planned design encrypts data at rest and in transit, with on-premise and air-gapped options for enterprise clients. These are design goals; no certification has been obtained yet.
Which nuclear codes does NeutrAI integrate with?
NeutrAI is being designed to work alongside MCNP, SERPENT, OpenMC, RELAP5-3D, TRACE, MAAP, SCALE, ORIGEN, and SIMULATE-3. No integrations are available yet.
Is there an academic / research discount?
It is planned. Universities and research laboratories are intended to get discounted access once the product launches.
Can I switch plans or cancel anytime?
Sign-up is not open yet. The plan is for monthly plans that can be changed or cancelled at the end of any billing cycle.
Does NeutrAI cover international regulatory frameworks?
That is the plan. The knowledge base is being designed to include IAEA Safety Standards, WENRA Reference Levels, EUR requirements, CNS/FANR/CNSC frameworks, and the regulatory regimes of all 30+ countries with operating nuclear programs.
